Privacy Policy

In plain words

This website is a personal site. It does not sell anything, does not show ads, and does not run analytics trackers. If this page exists, it is mostly so that a Google OAuth consent screen has something honest to link to.

What this site collects

  • Nothing on purpose. No tracking cookies, no fingerprinting, no third-party analytics.
  • Your hosting provider may log routine technical data (IP address, user agent, time of request) as part of normal operation. That is standard internet plumbing, not a surveillance program.

The related OAuth app (rclone Google Drive sync)

I run a personal Google Drive sync using the open-source tool rclone, registered with a Google OAuth client under my own account. In that context:

  • The app accesses only my own Google Drive, in read/write mode, to keep my devices in sync.
  • It is not offered to other users and does not share or sell any data — there is no data to share.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on my own machines, used solely to talk to the Google Drive API.
  • Google's own privacy policy applies to everything processed by Google.

Contact

Questions? Reach me at the support email shown on the OAuth consent screen — it goes to a mailbox I actually read.
